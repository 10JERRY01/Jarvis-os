Privacy Policy & Transparency

Jarvis OS Operating System

The Jarvis OS Linux distribution itself enforces the Wi-Fi-Off Law: it contains zero telemetry, zero outbound ad tracking, and executes local AI inference strictly on your own hardware (127.0.0.1).

Public Project Website (GitHub Pages)

When consented, privacy-first developer advertising (EthicalAds or Carbon Ads) may display contextual technical sponsorships to help cover ISO hosting infrastructure. ISO files are served via Cloudflare R2 and GitHub Releases.
